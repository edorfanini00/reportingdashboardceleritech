The next useful step is not always a meeting. If your team is still confirming ownership, scope, or timeline, this page gives you something practical to take back internally.

FIRST

Write down the ECC/R/3 dependency.

Name the business process that would break if the system moved too fast. Finance close, warehouse, production, procurement: pick the real one.

SECOND

Put a date beside the pressure.

A migration with a date is a project. A migration without a date is still discovery. Treat them differently.

THIRD

Bring the right person in early.

The best first call has someone who owns the system, someone who owns the process, and someone who can approve the next step.
